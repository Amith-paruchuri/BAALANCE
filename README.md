# BAALANCE • Hair Cortisol Chronobiology & Burnout Defense Platform

> **"Your hair keeps the receipts."**

BAALANCE is an endocrine-chronobiology clinical intelligence platform that correlates 90-day segmented hair cortisol spectrometry (ELISA) against live calendar workload telemetry and wearable sleep architecture.

Live Production URL: **[https://baalance.in](https://baalance.in)**

---

## Features

- **Segmented Hair Cortisol Chronology**: 90-day retrospective cortisol quantification across 1.0 cm hair segments (Hair Tip, Mid-Shaft, and Scalp Root).
- **AI Document Verification & ELISA Parser**: Gemini 2.5 Flash document scanning engine that authenticates genuine hair cortisol laboratory reports and rejects invalid score reports or non-hair tests.
- **Calendar Curfew Defense**: Automated scanner detecting evening meeting breaches past 7:00 PM, proposing daytime alternative slots, updating schedule in real-time, and dispatching invitee notifications.
- **Biomarker-Calendar Discordance**: Clinically isolates non-work somatic stress drivers when severe cortisol surges coexist with low meeting hours.
- **Tricha AI Copilot**: Clinical endocrine assistant powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash with live biomarker grounding.
